Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version

  • We collect what you give us (account and profile details, bookmarks, squads, messages) and how you use the app (analytics and session recordings).
  • We use it to run OneStop and make it better. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Your WhatsApp number is only shared with squad members you accept.
  • You can delete your account and its data from your profile at any time.

1. Who we are

OneStop (onestop.two19labs.in) is a competition discovery and team-finding platform for students, run by Two19 Labs ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use OneStop, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Questions or requests about your data can be sent to two19labs@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password when you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored only in hashed form. We never see them.
  • Google sign-in: if you continue with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password or access to your Google account beyond this basic profile.
  • Profile details: college, course, year, education level, skills, bio, profile photo and WhatsApp number.
  • Content and activity: competitions you bookmark, squad posts you create, squad applications you send or receive, messages you send in squad and competition chats, and your notification preferences.

Information collected automatically

  • Product analytics: we use PostHog to understand how OneStop is used, such as screens viewed, buttons clicked, features used, and approximate device, browser and location information derived from your IP address.
  • Session recordings: PostHog records how you interact with pages (mouse movements, clicks, scrolling and what appears on screen, including text you type into most fields) so we can find bugs and improve the experience. Password and phone number fields are masked and not recorded.
  • Error logs: technical information when something breaks, such as browser console messages.

Information stored on your device

  • We use your browser's local storage and cookies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, cache competition and deadline data for faster loading, and identify your browser for analytics.
  • If you turn on browser notifications, your browser gives us a push subscription so we can send deadline reminders. You can turn this off in your browser settings at any time.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and keep it secure.
  • To show you competitions that fit your college, year and interests.
  • To track deadlines for competitions you bookmark and send you reminders and alerts.
  • To run the team finder: showing squad posts, handling applications, and enabling chat between squad members.
  • To understand how OneStop is used, fix problems and improve features.
  • To respond to your messages and requests (for example, a request to add your college).
  • To prevent abuse, spam and misuse of the platform.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run OneStop. They process data on our behalf and only for the purposes described here:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and user authentication.
  • Google: "Continue with Google" sign-in.
  • PostHog: product analytics and session recordings (data stored in the United States).
  • Vercel: website hosting and delivery.

Because these providers operate internationally, your information may be stored and processed outside India.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or Two19 Labs.

5. How long we keep your information

  • We keep your account and profile information for as long as your account is active.
  • Squad posts are removed automatically after they expire.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, bookmarks, squad posts, squad applications, chat messages you sent and notifications.
  • Analytics and session recording data is kept by PostHog according to its retention settings, and is not used to identify you after your account is deleted.

6. Your rights and choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can:

  • Access and correct your information by editing your profile in the app, or by contacting us.
  • Delete your account and its data using Delete account on your profile page. If you can't do this in the app (for example, if you signed up with Google), email us and we will delete it for you.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account or by contacting us. This does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew.
  • Turn off notifications in the app or in your browser settings.
  • Raise a complaint with us at the contact below. If you're not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls that limit who can read each type of data, and hashed passwords. No online service is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects you.

8. Age requirement

OneStop is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as OneStop changes. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app or by email.
